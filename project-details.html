Architectural Monograph

Nexus Global Headquarters

A computational double-skin commercial high-rise pioneering daylight autonomy and biophilic sky lounges in London.

Home / Portfolio / Nexus HQ
Programmatic Overview

The Convergence of Biophilic Light & Structural Rigor

Commissioned as the international headquarters for a next-generation fintech conglomerate, the Nexus Tower redefines the London skyline with an organic twisting profile generated via algorithmic wind pressure mapping.

The 42-story crystalline envelope incorporates triple-glazed parametric apertures that automatically actuate in response to exterior temperature differentials, drastically reducing artificial cooling requirements while bathing interior trading floors in diffuse daylight.

Computational Design Methodology

Using our proprietary neural topology algorithms, the structural grid was optimized to eliminate 32% of interior column load points, creating vast 2,400 m² column-free floor plates that foster seamless cross-team collaboration.

Key Technical Innovations

  • Closed-Loop Geothermal: 180 deep borehole heat exchangers supplying 100% of heating and cooling baseline.
  • Biophilic Sky Lounges: Four triple-height botanical gardens acting as natural air scrubbers on intermediate floors.
  • Atmospheric Condensate Harvesters: Capturing over 450,000 liters of gray water annually for vertical living walls.
  • LEED Platinum & WELL Certified: Surpassing international decarbonization and occupant wellness benchmarks.

Active Research Domains

Six Pillars of Architectural Innovation

Investigating the intersection of synthetic biology, computational physics, and net-zero engineering.

Program 01

Generative Parametric AI

Leveraging deep neural generative networks to test millions of structural topology variants in minutes, optimizing wind resistance and natural day-lighting.

Program 02

Carbon-Negative Biomaterials

Synthesizing mycelium insulation, structural hempcrete, and bio-cement composites that actively sequester atmospheric CO2 during the building's lifecycle.

Program 03

Kinetic Biomimetic Facades

Developing mechanically responsive sunshades inspired by pinecone seed dispersal that open and close autonomously with ambient thermal differentials.

Program 04

Atmospheric Water Harvesting

Integrating passive dew collection and condensation channels within building facades to supply high-altitude vertical gardens without external irrigation.

Program 05

Robotic Concrete 3D Printing

Employing multi-axis autonomous robotic arms to deposit reinforced concrete only where structural stress exists, slashing material waste by over 45%.

Program 06

Urban Microclimate Simulation

Modeling computational fluid dynamics (CFD) across city districts to eliminate heat island phenomena and optimize pedestrian cooling corridors.
